Privacy Policy — Homebridge Nest

Homebridge Nest is a personal integration for my own household. It connects my own Google Nest thermostat, cameras, and doorbell to Apple Home through Homebridge, using the Google Smart Device Management API.

Effective November 15, 2026.

What data is accessed

  • Device information and status for my Nest thermostat, cameras, and doorbell (for example, current temperature, thermostat settings, and camera motion or person events).
  • Camera livestreams and snapshots only when I view them in Apple Home.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to show and control my own devices in Apple Home. Google sign-in tokens are stored locally on my home Mac mini, in the Homebridge configuration. No data is sold, shared with, or sent to any third party. No analytics are collected.
